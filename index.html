Why this exists

Most contract templates online are either a paywalled PDF, a 40-page legal document written for a law firm, or a Word file with broken formatting. If you are a freelancer invoicing a few thousand pounds, none of those fit.

So these are short by design. Each one covers the clauses that decide who pays whom, who owns what and how either side gets out — and leaves out the boilerplate nobody reads.

The documents are generated in your browser, which means your client's address, your rates and your bank details are never uploaded anywhere.

How to use one

  1. Fill the form. The preview updates as you type; the highlighted blanks are what is still missing.
  2. Read it before you send it. Replace anything that does not match how you actually work.
  3. Download as .doc to edit in Word or Google Docs, or print to PDF for a fixed copy.
  4. Both sides sign. For a UK contract, a typed name in an email exchange can be enough, but a signature block is clearer evidence.
Not legal advice

These are general-purpose templates. High-value deals, unusual industries and anything involving property or employment should be checked by a qualified solicitor or attorney.

Common questions

Are these documents legally binding?

A signed agreement between two parties is generally binding if it meets the basic requirements of contract law where you are. These templates are written to be clear and balanced, but they are general information rather than legal advice. For anything high-value, have a professional review the finished document.

Does the site store my information?

No. Everything is generated in your browser. Drafts are saved to your own browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off, and nothing is uploaded to a server.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup, no email capture and no paywall on any generator.

UK or US?

The contract and NDA generators let you choose the governing law, including England and Wales, Scotland, Northern Ireland and common US states. The invoice generator handles UK VAT and US sales tax fields.

Why is it free?

The site is supported by advertising. There are no affiliate links inside the documents themselves, and the generators stay free.
