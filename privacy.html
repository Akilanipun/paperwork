Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. This page explains what is stored, what is not, and what the advertising partners do.

The short version

  • Your document contents never leave your browser. The generators run as JavaScript on your device. There is no upload endpoint, no server-side processing and no database of user documents.
  • Drafts are stored locally in your browser's localStorage, so you can close the tab and come back. Clearing site data or pressing “Start over” deletes them.
  • Advertising cookies are optional. Ads only load after you accept the banner, and every tool works if you decline.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for an account, an email address or a phone number. We do not sell documents, contact details or usage profiles. We do not have a mailing list.

Local storage

Two kinds of entry are saved in your browser: form values for each generator (key prefix paperwork:doc:), checklist ticks (paperwork:check:), and your cookie choice (consent). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted. You can delete it at any time through your browser's site data settings.

Advertising

This site is free because it carries advertising. A single display banner is served by Adsterra, a third-party ad network. When you accept the banner, Adsterra loads its script and may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure and personalise that ad. Adsterra's privacy policy is at adsterra.com/privacy-policy.

If you decline, no advertising script is requested and no advertising cookie is set. Every generator and checklist still works, because the documents are built entirely in your browser.

You can control advertising cookies through Your Online Choices (Europe) or aboutads.info (US). If Google AdSense is enabled in future, its advertising policy and Ads Settings page will apply instead.

To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings and the banner will appear again.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider (see below), which may record standard technical logs — IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp — for security and abuse prevention. Those logs are generated by the host, not by us, and are not linked to any document you create.

Your rights

If you are in the UK or EU, the UK GDPR and EU GDPR give you rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability over personal data. Because we do not hold a database of user data, most requests concern advertising identifiers, which are controlled by the ad provider. You can exercise rights against that provider directly, or contact us and we will point you to the right place.

If you are a California resident, the CCPA/CPRA gives you rights to know, delete, correct and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Opting out of advertising cookies through the banner prevents the sharing that would otherwise occur for personalised ads.

Children

The site is intended for adults running a business or managing their own tax affairs. It is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change. Material changes — a new advertising provider, for example — will be reflected here before they take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@sltechempire.store.
