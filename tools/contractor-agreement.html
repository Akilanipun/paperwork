Why the wording matters more in the US

In the UK, the distinction between a freelancer and an employee is mostly about tax and employment rights. In the US, misclassification can trigger back taxes, penalties, unpaid overtime and a state-level audit. The agreement cannot decide the question on its own — how the relationship actually operates does — but it is the first thing an auditor or court reads.

So this template documents the facts that support contractor status: the contractor controls how the work is done, supplies their own equipment, can work for other clients, is paid per project or per hour rather than a salary, receives no benefits, and handles their own taxes. It also asks for a W-9 and explains the 1099-NEC reporting threshold.

What is in the agreement

  • Services and term — what is being delivered, and whether the engagement is open-ended or has an end date.
  • Compensation — fixed fee or hourly, invoicing, Net 7/15/30/45, and whether expenses are reimbursable.
  • Independent contractor status — the control, equipment, tax and benefits language that documents the relationship.
  • Intellectual property — work made for hire plus an assignment on payment, or a licence if the contractor keeps ownership.
  • Confidentiality, representations and liability — including a cap equal to the fees paid.
  • Termination and governing law — notice period and the state whose law applies.

State law is not uniform

California, Massachusetts and New Jersey use the ABC test, which is much stricter: a worker is presumed to be an employee unless the hiring business can show all three conditions, including that the work is outside its usual course of business. Other states use a multi-factor test closer to the federal economic reality test. Some states also require written contracts for certain industries, or cap non-compete terms. If you are hiring in California, read the ABC test before you start.

Not legal advice

Worker classification is fact-specific and penalties are significant. If you are hiring regularly, or the role looks like a permanent full-time position, get advice from an employment attorney in your state.

Questions

What makes someone a contractor rather than an employee?

There is no single test. Courts look at control, opportunity for profit or loss, equipment, whether the person can work for others, and how they are paid. Several states use the stricter ABC test, which presumes employment unless all three parts are met. A contract documents your intent but cannot override how the relationship actually works.

When do I need a 1099-NEC?

Businesses generally issue Form 1099-NEC to non-employee contractors paid $600 or more in a calendar year, filed by 31 January. Collect a W-9 before paying anyone.

What is work made for hire?

It means the client owns the copyright from creation. US law applies it automatically to employees and to certain commissioned categories only. For most freelance design or code, you need an explicit assignment clause — which this template includes.

Does it need to be notarised?

No. A signed agreement is generally enforceable without a notary. E-signature platforms are useful for audit trails but a signed PDF is normally enough.

Can I use this for a UK contractor?

Use the UK freelance contract instead. It covers the UK-specific points — statutory late payment interest, IR35-adjacent status language and UK GDPR — which this US document does not.
