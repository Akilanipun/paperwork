Why checklists beat guides

A guide explains the rules. A checklist makes sure you actually did the thing. Tax and company formation both fail in the same way: not because the rules are hard, but because one step gets missed and everything downstream is wrong. A missing UTR means you cannot file. A missing EIN means you cannot open a bank account. A missed annual report dissolves the company.

Each checklist is written in the order you actually do the work, with the non-obvious items explained in a note you can expand.
