About Paperwork

Free document generators and checklists for people who do their own admin.

What this site is

Paperwork turns a short form into a usable business document: a freelance contract, an invoice, a mutual NDA, an independent contractor agreement. It also publishes checklists for jobs where the order of steps matters — a UK self-assessment return, a US LLC formation.

Everything runs in your browser. There is no account, no email capture, no upload endpoint and no database. The only thing stored is a draft in your own browser's local storage, and you can clear it with the “Start over” button.

Who it is for

  • Freelancers and consultants who need a contract before the client's procurement team asks for one.
  • Sole traders who want an invoice that satisfies UK VAT rules without a subscription to accounting software.
  • Small businesses in the US hiring a contractor and needing the 1099 paperwork to be defensible.
  • Anyone who has read a 40-page template and given up.

How the documents are written

Each template is deliberately short and follows the same structure: what is being delivered, what it costs, who owns it, and how either side exits. Clauses that only exist to protect a law firm's fee are left out. Anything that varies by jurisdiction — late payment interest, governing law, worker classification — is either made a choice in the form or flagged in the guidance.

Content is reviewed periodically. The date at the bottom of each page records the last review. Tax thresholds, filing fees and statutory rates change, so the guidance tells you where to confirm the current figure rather than pretending a number is permanent.

Not legal advice

Paperwork is a document assembly tool, not a law firm. The templates are general information. For high-value contracts, property, employment or regulated industries, use a qualified solicitor or attorney.

How it is funded

The site carries display advertising. Ads are the only revenue: there are no affiliate links inside the documents, no paid placements in the tool results, and no upsell to a paid tier. If you decline advertising cookies, every generator still works.

Corrections and contact

If something in a template is wrong, out of date, or reads badly, tell us and it will be fixed. Email hello@sltechempire.store with the page and the wording you would change.
